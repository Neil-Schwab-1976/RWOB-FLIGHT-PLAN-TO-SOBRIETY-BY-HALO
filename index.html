Stressor-to-Colour Mapping

How each stressor identified in the questionnaire maps to WHMIS colours through the D.O.S.E. neurochemical framework. This is the intake bridge.

WHITE — Foundation

D.O.S.E.: Serotonin — baseline regulation

Stressors: Cognitive fog (PAWS H1), orientation uncertainty, HALT: Tired, structural drift, first-contact overwhelm

Modalities: Navigation, system orientation, harm reduction, housing stabilization, primary care

Serotonin

BLUE — Health & Depth

D.O.S.E.: Oxytocin — connection & depth

Stressors: Emotional overreaction/numbness (PAWS H2), unprocessed grief, enmeshment, interpersonal conflict, shame, HALT: Lonely

Modalities: Community counselling, outpatient, family therapy, grief work, concurrent-disorder, 2SLGBTQ2S+ care

Oxytocin

YELLOW — Reactivity & Response

D.O.S.E.: Endorphins — action & recovery

Stressors: Stress sensitivity (PAWS H4), cravings/craving confusion (PAWS H5), HALT: Angry, social pressure, cue reactivity

Modalities: Crisis support, withdrawal management, RAAM clinics, emergency response, gambling intervention

Endorphins

BLACK — Shadow & Transformation

D.O.S.E.: All four — full activation

Stressors: Identity confusion (PAWS H7), anhedonia (PAWS H6), sleep disruption (PAWS H3), mental relapse (CENAPS), cortisol dominance

Modalities: Residential treatment, hospital/inpatient, intensive addiction medicine, trauma/PTSD, long-term recovery

Full D.O.S.E.

RED — Emergency & Crisis

D.O.S.E.: Cortisol regulation

Stressors: Physical relapse stage (CENAPS), acute crisis, immediate danger, suicidal ideation

Modalities: Emergency stabilization, mobile crisis response, hospital diversion, 24/7 hotlines

Same-Denominator Principle: The planet needs restoration from WHITE through GOLD. The human needs restoration from WHITE through GOLD. The work is the same work.
